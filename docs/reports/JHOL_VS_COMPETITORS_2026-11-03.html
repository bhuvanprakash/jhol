Wins that matter for real work

  • gc_stress — 2M short-lived allocations: 10.0 ms. QuickJS 694 (69x), node 98, bun 13.7.
  • iter_sum — 50M-iteration loop: 38.5 ms. node 382.8 (9.9x), QuickJS 1090 (28x), JSC 312 (8x).
  • polymorphic_props — shapes churn, inline caches hold: 14.0 vs bun 16.4.
  • sort_bench — LCG fill + 100k sort: 22.8 vs bun 29.4, node 122.9.

The honest side of the ledger

  • json_roundtrip, method_calls, object_props, string_build: bun wins these 20–40%. Parity overall is not uniform dominance.
  • fib_recursive is constant-folded (literal argument) — that row measures startup, not recursion. With live args the JIT runs 126.5M calls in 0.14 s (under bun); introduce a side effect and the same work costs 4.46 s (~35 ns/call, interpreter fallback). One language, three regimes: folded · JIT · interpreter.
  • that fallback is the one open gap; tracked in docs/engineering/PRODUCTION_READY_2026-11-03.md.
