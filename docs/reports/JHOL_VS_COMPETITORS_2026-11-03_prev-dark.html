Wins that matter for real work

  • gc_stress, 2M short-lived allocs: 10.0 ms — QuickJS 694 ms (69x), node 98 ms, bun 13.7 ms.
  • iter_sum, 50M-iteration loop: 38.5 ms vs node 382.8 (9.9x), QuickJS 1090 (28x), JSC 312 (8x).
  • polymorphic_props: 14.0 vs bun 16.4 — inline caches hold under shape churn.
  • sort_bench, LCG + 100k sort: 22.8 vs bun 29.4, node 122.9.

The honest footnote

  • Orange json_roundtrip / method_calls / object_props / string_build: bun wins these 20–40%. Parity overall ≠ uniform dominance.
  • fib_recursive is constant-folded (literal argument) — that row measures startup, not recursion. With a runtime arg the JIT runs 126.5M calls in 0.14 s, faster than bun; with a captured counter, 4.46 s (~35 ns/call). One language, three regimes: folded, JIT, interpreter.
  • Interpreter fallback (26x vs bun) is the remaining gap. Tracked, not hidden.
